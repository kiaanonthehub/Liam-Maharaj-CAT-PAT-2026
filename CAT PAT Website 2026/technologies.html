Mitigation

Solar and wind energy

Renewable sources emit little to no greenhouse gases during operation and can replace fossil-fuel electricity.

  • Advantages: low operating emissions, widely available resources, improved energy security.
  • Challenges: weather variability, upfront costs, storage and grid requirements.
  • Assessment: strongest large-scale potential when supported by storage.
Cleaner transport

Electric vehicles

Electric vehicles use batteries and motors and produce no tailpipe emissions while running on electricity.

  • Advantages: cleaner local air and high energy efficiency.
  • Challenges: price, charging access, charging time and range concerns.
  • Assessment: most effective when charged with renewable electricity.
Circular economy

Recycling technology

Sorting and recovery systems reduce landfill waste and lower demand for newly extracted materials.

  • Advantages: conserves resources and reduces waste.
  • Challenges: correct separation, infrastructure and public participation.
  • Assessment: an important supporting solution.
Adaptation

Climate-smart agriculture

Climate information, efficient water management and appropriate technologies can improve resilience and food security.

  • Advantages: better water use, planning and resilience.
  • Challenges: solutions must suit local conditions and require knowledge and finance.
  • Assessment: especially important for drought, heat and changing rainfall.
Behaviour change

Digital tools

Smart meters, connected devices and mobile applications can give feedback about energy use and support better decisions.

  • Advantages: immediate information and improved awareness.
  • Challenges: information only helps when users act and technology is accessible.
  • Assessment: strengthens other solutions rather than replacing them.
Enabling solution

Battery storage

Storage can save renewable electricity for use when sunlight or wind is unavailable.

  • Advantages: improves reliability and supports electric transport.
  • Challenges: cost, materials and responsible end-of-life management.
  • Assessment: essential for a more flexible clean-energy system.

Ten questions that guided the investigation

Renewable energy questions 1–4
