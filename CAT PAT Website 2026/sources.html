Glossary

Key terms used in the project

Mitigation
Reducing greenhouse-gas emissions or increasing their removal.
Adaptation
Adjusting to climate impacts and increasing resilience.
Renewable energy
Energy from resources such as sunlight and wind that are naturally replenished.
Life-cycle emissions
Emissions linked to production, use and end-of-life stages.
Climate-smart agriculture
An approach that connects food security, adaptation and mitigation.
Circular economy
Keeping materials in use through repair, reuse and recycling.
