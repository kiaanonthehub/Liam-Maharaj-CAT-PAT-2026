Focus question
How is modern technology helping to reduce the effects of climate change, and which solutions are the most effective?
Introduction

Why this investigation matters

Climate change is linked to greenhouse-gas emissions from human activities. This project evaluates practical technologies according to their effectiveness, advantages, limitations and ability to support mitigation or adaptation.

🔎

Research

Evidence was gathered from established international and government organisations and a research publication.

📊

Data analysis

A 15-response practice dataset was analysed using spreadsheet calculations, percentages and four meaningful graphs.

🎯

Purpose

The findings answer the focus question and support clear recommendations for a more sustainable future.

Smart farming

Information, sensors and efficient irrigation improve resilience.

Main conclusion

The best response is a system, not a single device

Renewable energy has the strongest large-scale potential. The overall climate benefit improves when clean electricity is combined with storage, electric transport, recycling, climate-smart agriculture and informed user behaviour.
