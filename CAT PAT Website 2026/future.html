Roadmap

Four connected steps

Clean power

Solar and wind, supported by storage, provide low-emission electricity.

Electric mobility

Vehicles and public transport use cleaner electricity and remove local tailpipe emissions.

Smart resources

Schools, homes and farms monitor electricity and water to reduce waste.

Circular systems

Products and materials are repaired, reused, recycled and managed responsibly.

Recommendations

Actions supported by the evidence

1

Prioritise renewables

Expand affordable solar and wind together with storage and grid improvements.

2

Reduce the cost barrier

Use incentives, shared infrastructure and programmes that show long-term savings.

3

Build understanding

Teach users how technologies work and how to use them effectively.

4

Combine technologies

Link clean power, EVs, recycling, smart farming and digital monitoring.

Final answer to the focus question
Modern technology reduces climate-change effects through cleaner energy and transport, waste reduction, resource efficiency and adaptation. Renewable energy, especially solar power, has the strongest large-scale potential, but the most effective response is an affordable combination of technologies.

Personal action

What a school community can do

Measure

Track electricity, water and waste to identify where improvements are possible.

Reduce

Use efficient lighting, switch off unused devices and separate recyclable waste.

Demonstrate

Use small renewable-energy or water-saving projects to teach through practical examples.
